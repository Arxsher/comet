FAQ

Questions, answered.

Something else on your mind? Open a room and ask — we’ll see you typing.

Yes — that’s the whole point. Everyone in the room sees your line as you type it, typos and backspaces included. Enter moves it into the conversation; Esc clears it, though people may have already seen it.

No. Pick a display name and a colour when you join. Midsentence remembers them in your browser for next time — nothing else.

Link-only rooms are end-to-end encrypted: your browser encrypts every keystroke with a key derived from the room link before it leaves your device, so only people with the link can read it. Public rooms are listed on this page, so anyone can join and read them — keep secrets in link-only rooms.

Only the recent part of a conversation is kept while a room is in use, so people who join late can catch up. Shortly after the last person leaves, the room’s history is cleared.

Midsentence reconnects on its own and keeps your seat. Anything you commit while offline is sent as soon as you’re back.
